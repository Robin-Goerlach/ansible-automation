# Share a diagnostic bundle

1. Build the bundle only from the required run ID.
2. Verify the external manifest and archive SHA-256.
3. Extract into a private temporary directory.
4. Search for customer data, credentials, tokens, cookies, private keys, and unrelated hosts.
5. Remove unnecessary files and rebuild through the approved process when needed.
6. Transfer through an approved encrypted channel.
7. Record recipient, purpose, retention, and deletion requirements.

Automatic key-name redaction does not make a bundle suitable for public issue trackers.
